# Body web: an idea (2026-11-12)

Martin's idea. Not agreed for building yet; nothing here goes in the game
until it's moved into a PLAN.md. It may become its own game rather than
part of Pack. That's still to decide.

Labels as in PLAN.md: **DECIDED** (Martin's call), **PLACEHOLDER** (mine),
**OPEN** (a question still to answer).

---

## The idea

The base monster is simpler and the aspects matter more. An aspect doesn't
just tweak attacks: it **reshapes the body**.

- A **spiderweb graph** (a radar chart) of the body's parts. Each aspect
  pick pushes some parts up and others down.
- At level 2 or 3, an aspect can **completely remodel a part**.
  Martin's examples:
  - BLADES 2: bigger arms with big scything talons.
  - BLADES 3: the wings become two extra blade arms.
  - BLUNT 3: the wings are removed entirely.

## The base moves

DECIDED (as part of this idea)
- Four base moves:
  - BITE (head);
  - HOP: the back hop only, with no jump attack;
  - TAIL SWIPE (the tail whip);
  - HIP CHECK (body).
- That means the leap slam, the arm combo and the charge are not base
  moves. They could come back as aspect moves.

## The web

Each spoke is a part of the body. The raptor's parts: HEAD, ARMS, WINGS,
BODY, TAIL, LEGS (the raptor's art already has wings).

OPEN
- Which parts are spokes? (Proposed: the six above.)
- What a part's value does. Some options:
  - bigger = its moves hit harder and reach further;
  - bigger = more health or armour on that part (if hunters' hits land on
    parts);
  - smaller = lighter and quicker (wind-ups, hop distance).
- Where a run starts: every spoke the same, or a raptor shape?
- Does a part that goes to zero disappear (as BLUNT 3 removes the wings)?
  Do its moves go with it?
- How much one aspect level moves the web, and does it ever go down by
  itself (as opposed to only when you take another aspect)?
- Are stats (STRENGTH / SPEED / DEFENSE) replaced by the web? (They do
  similar jobs: a stronger body part is like more strength for its moves.)

## Remodels

OPEN
- Which levels remodel: level 2 or level 3 only, or both?
- Does a remodel replace that part's move, change it, or add one? (Talon
  arms might turn a swipe into a scything combo; blade wings might add a
  new attack.)
- Do two aspects fight over the same part? (BLADES 3 turns the wings into
  blade arms; BLUNT 3 removes them. If you have both, which wins?)
- How it shows: the creature itself changes (decided before: what an
  aspect grows shows on the creature). The puppet rig will need the new
  parts drawn.

## How this fits what's built (Pack-free)

- Built and still fits: bite, hop, tail whip, hip check, solid bodies,
  knockback, stagger, the levelling screens, the start setup, bleed.
- Would move from base to aspect: the leap slam, the arm combo, the charge
  (all built; they'd be unlocked by aspects instead).
- BLADES 1 and 2 as built change the arm combo and the charge. Under this
  idea they'd be redone around the web and the remodels.
